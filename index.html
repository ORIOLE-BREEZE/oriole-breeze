Our Story

Oriole Breeze began at home, with curiosity. It started with a simple question: what if fragrance could feel more natural, more personal, and less overwhelming? From experimenting with flowers and fruit peels to creating our own fragrance blends, every scent has grown from that same curiosity. Handmade in small batches, Oriole Breeze is about finding a fragrance that feels like you.
